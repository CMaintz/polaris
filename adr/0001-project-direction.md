# ADR 0001 - Project Direction

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Project Polaris is a LinkedIn-first intelligence and decision-support product designed to evolve toward a broader growth platform.

The current product form is a web application.

## Context

The project originally targeted a local Windows/macOS application to avoid hosting costs and keep data local. Cross-device accessibility has since become the preferred product direction.

The privacy and user-ownership principles from the local-first concept remain important.
